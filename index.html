Cyberpunk 2077 Save Editor

CyberCAT-SimpleGUI Co-maintainer

I didn't start this one. Deweh wrote it in 2021 on top of CyberCAT, and seberoth kept it working through the 2.x patches until mid-2025. I forked it to fill in what was missing, and while upstreaming fixes via PR, seberoth invited me and topagae to join the official Nexus Mods team and take over maintenance.

Because Deweh is inactive, active development continues in this repo - now with a Cyberpunk HUD, save browser, ripperdoc cyberware editor, build export/import, cloud save sync, tests and CI.

Windows desktop · C# · .NET 10 · WinForms · v0.38
